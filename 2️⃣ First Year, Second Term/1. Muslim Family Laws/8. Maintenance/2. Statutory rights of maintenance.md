## 8.2 Statutory Rights of Maintenance ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (5/5):** While Sharia creates the _duty_, these statutes create the _remedy_. You cannot be a lawyer in Bangladesh without knowing how to actually file a maintenance case under these laws.
    
- **Exam (5/5):** A guaranteed practical topic. Questions often ask for the "Procedure for recovery of maintenance under MFLO 1961" or compare the Family Court remedy with the Arbitration Council remedy.
    

---

### The Enforcement Machinery

In Bangladesh, a Muslim wife does not have to rely solely on the moral conscience of her husband. She has powerful statutory weapons to enforce her right to _Nafaqah_.

#### 1. The Muslim Family Laws Ordinance (MFLO), 1961 (Section 9)

This is the administrative (fast-track) remedy.

- **The Provision:** Section 9 explicitly states that if any husband fails to maintain his wife adequately (or fails to maintain co-wives equitably), the wife can apply to the **Chairman** of the Arbitration Council.
    
- **The Procedure:**
    
    1. **Application:** The wife submits a written application to the Chairman (Union Parishad/Municipality).
        
    2. **Arbitration Council:** The Chairman forms a Council (Chairman + Rep of Wife + Rep of Husband).
        
    3. **Decision:** The Council determines whether maintenance is due and fixes the **amount**.
        
    4. **Certificate:** The Chairman issues a certificate specifying the amount.
        
- **Recovery:** If the husband fails to pay the certified amount, it is recoverable as **"Arrears of Land Revenue"** (a harsh state mechanism involving certificate officers who can attach property or arrest).
    
- **Revision:** The husband can file a revision against the Council's decision to the Assistant Judge within 30 days.
    

#### 2. The Family Courts Ordinance, 1985 (The Civil Remedy)

This is the judicial (standard) remedy and the most common route used today.

- **Jurisdiction:** Under **Section 5**, the Family Court has **exclusive jurisdiction** to try suits for maintenance.
    
- **The Advantage:**
    
    - **Past Maintenance:** Unlike the MFLO Council (which usually looks forward), the Family Court has the clear power to decree **past maintenance** (arrears) for the period the wife was neglected before filing the suit.
        
    - **Enforcement:** The Court can enforce its own decree through money execution cases.
        
- **Forum:** The wife files the suit in the Family Court (Assistant Judge's Court) where she resides.
    

#### 3. Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Section 488) - _Historical Context_

- **The Old Law:** Historically, wives used Section 488 of the CrPC to claim maintenance in Magistrate Courts. It was a "quasi-criminal" speedy remedy to prevent vagrancy.
    
- **Current Status:** Since the establishment of Family Courts in 1985, Section 488 has largely become **obsolete** for Muslim wives in Bangladesh, as the Family Courts Ordinance overrides it for Muslims. Lawyers generally do not use this section anymore for Muslim clients.
    

#### 4. Pita-Matar Bharan-Poshan Ain, 2013 (Parents' Maintenance)

While Topic 8 usually focuses on the wife, the syllabus includes "Statutory rights" broadly. This Act created a statutory right for **Parents** to claim maintenance.

- **Obligation:** Children must maintain elderly parents.
    
- **Penalty:** Failure is a criminal offense punishable by fines (1 Lakh BDT) or imprisonment.
    
- **Forum:** Judicial Magistrate Court (not Family Court).

### Summary Table: Which Route to Choose?

|**Feature**|**Arbitration Council (MFLO S.9)**|**Family Court (FCO 1985)**|
|---|---|---|
|**Nature**|Administrative / ADR.|Judicial / Civil Suit.|
|**Speed**|Faster (theoretically).|Slower (Full trial procedure).|
|**Past Maintenance**|Generally NO (focuses on current/future).|**YES** (Can claim up to 6 years past).|
|**Enforcement**|As Land Revenue (Certificate Case).|Execution Decree (Civil Process).|
|**Best For...**|Small amounts, quick settlement.|Large arrears, complex disputes, or divorce + dower claims together.|

### Important Exam Tip

**"Double Jeopardy?"**

- _Question:_ Can a wife file for maintenance under Section 9 (Chairman) AND in the Family Court at the same time?
    
- _Answer:_ **NO.** She must choose one forum. If she fails in the Arbitration Council, she cannot simply re-file the same claim in Family Court (Res Judicata principles apply). However, she usually prefers the **Family Court** because it allows her to claim **Past Maintenance**, which is often a large sum of money.
