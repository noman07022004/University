## 8.3 Maintenance During Iddat ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (5/5):** This defines the final financial obligation of the husband. It is critical to understand _why_ a divorced woman gets paid while waiting, but a widow generally does not.
    
- **Exam (5/5):** A favorite trap for students. Examiners will ask: _"A husband dies. Does the widow get maintenance during her 4 months 10 days Iddat?"_ (Hint: The answer is usually No).
    

---

###  The Final Obligation

The right to _Nafaqah_ (Maintenance) during the _Iddat_ period depends entirely on **how the marriage ended** (Divorce vs. Death).

#### 1. In Case of Divorce (_Talaq_)

- **The Rule:** A divorced wife is **absolutely entitled** to maintenance during the period of _Iddat_.
    
- **Reasoning:** During the _Iddat_ of divorce, the marriage is technically considered to be "continuing" in a limited sense (especially in revocable divorce where the husband can take her back). Since she is prohibited from marrying anyone else because of him, he must pay for her upkeep.
    
- **Duration:**
    
    - **Menstruating:** For 3 menstrual courses (approx. 3 months).
        
    - **Pregnant:** Until the **delivery of the child** (even if it takes 9 months).
        
    - **Statutory (Bangladesh):** Under MFLO 1961, since the divorce is suspended for **90 days**, she is entitled to maintenance for those full 90 days.
        
- **Post-Iddat:** As confirmed by the Appellate Division in the _Hefzur Rahman_ case, the duty **ends strictly** when the Iddat expires. There is no lifetime maintenance.
    

#### 2. In Case of Death of Husband

This is the most common exam error.

- **The Rule:** A widow is generally **NOT entitled** to maintenance during the _Iddat_ of death (4 months 10 days).
    
- **Reasoning:**
    
    1. **Inheritance Replaces Maintenance:** Upon the husband's death, the wife becomes an heir (inheriting 1/8 or 1/4 of his estate). The law presumes she will support herself from this inheritance.
        
    2. **Property Transmission:** Maintenance is a debt against the husband. Since he is dead, his property has immediately transferred to the heirs. You cannot claim "maintenance" from property that now technically belongs to the heirs (including herself).
        
- **Exception:** If she is pregnant, some schools allow maintenance, but the general Hanafi view remains that her share in the inheritance covers her needs.
    

---

### Comparison Table: Divorce vs. Death

|**Feature**|**Iddat of Divorce**|**Iddat of Death**|
|---|---|---|
|**Entitlement**|**YES** (Absolute Right).|**NO** (General Rule).|
|**Reason**|Marriage technically subsists; she is restricted by him.|She becomes an heir (Owner).|
|**Duration**|3 Cycles / 90 Days / Until Delivery.|4 Months 10 Days / Until Delivery.|
|**Who Pays?**|The Husband.|No one (She uses her Inheritance).|

### Important Exam Tip

**"The Pregnant Widow"**

- **Scenario:** A widow is pregnant. Does she get maintenance?
    
- **Hanafi Law:** **No.** She inherits a share of the estate. The unborn child (once born) will also inherit a share. She must support herself from her share.
    
- **Contrast:** A _divorced_ pregnant wife **DOES** get maintenance because she does not inherit any property from the ex-husband.
