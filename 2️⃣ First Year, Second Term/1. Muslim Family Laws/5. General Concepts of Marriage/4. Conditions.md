## 5.5 Witness (_Shahadat_) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (⭐ 5/5):** The requirement of witnesses is what legally distinguishes a valid marriage from "secret cohabitation" or _Zina_ (adultery). It is the mechanism of **Publicity**.
    
- **Exam (⭐ 4.5/5):** A critical area of difference between Sunni and Shia law. Exam questions often ask: _"Are witnesses essential for a Muslim marriage?"_ or _"Compare the law of witnesses under Hanafi and Shia schools."_
    

---

###  The Requirement of Publicity

In Islamic Law, marriage is a social contract that must be publicly recognized. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) said: _"There is no marriage without a guardian and two just witnesses."_

However, the legal necessity of witnesses varies drastically between the schools.

#### 1. The Sunni (Hanafi) Law – Applicable in Bangladesh

In the **Hanafi School**, witnesses are an absolute **Condition of Validity** (_Shart_) for the marriage contract. The logic is that the contract must be public to protect the rights of the wife and prevent secret illicit relationships.

**A. Number and Gender:** The marriage must be contracted in the presence of:

- **Two Male** witnesses; OR
    
- **One Male and Two Female** witnesses.
    
- _Note:_ The testimony of women alone (e.g., four women) is **not accepted** for marriage in Hanafi law. There must be at least one male.
    

**B. Qualifications of Witnesses:**

1. **Sanity (_Aql_):** Must be of sound mind.
    
2. **Majority (_Bulugh_):** Must be adults (puberty/15+).
    
3. **Muslim Faith:** If both parties are Muslim, witnesses must be Muslim. (If the bride is Christian/Jewish, Hanafis allow Kitabi witnesses, but this is rare).
    
4. **Hearing:** They must hear the Offer and Acceptance simultaneously.
    

**C. Effect of Absence:**

- If a Hanafi couple marries without witnesses, the marriage is **NOT Void (_Batil_)**; it is **Irregular (_Fasid_)**.
    
- _Remedy:_ The defect is cured by simply acknowledging the marriage in front of witnesses later or contracting it again. The children born in an irregular marriage are **Legitimate**.
    

#### 2. The Shia Law (Ithna Ashari)

The Shia view is arguably the opposite of the Sunni view regarding witnesses.

- **Marriage:** Witnesses are **NOT required** for the validity of the marriage contract. A marriage contracted in private between a man and woman is perfectly **Valid (_Sahih_)**.
    
- **Divorce:** Ironically, Shias require **Two Witnesses** for the validity of **Divorce (_Talaq_)**.
    
    - _Contrast:_ Sunnis require witnesses for Marriage but _not_ for Divorce. Shias require witnesses for Divorce but _not_ for Marriage.
        

#### 3. The Maliki Law

- They focus on **Publicity (_I'lan_)**.
    
- Witnesses are not required at the exact moment of the contract (signing), but they are required before **Consummation** (sexual intercourse). If the couple makes the marriage public (by a feast/Walima) without formal witnesses at the signing, it is valid.
    

---

### 📝 Important Notes & Tips

- **Bangladeshi Context:**
    
    - Since Bangladesh follows Hanafi Law, witnesses are required.
        
    - **The Kabinnama:** The standard marriage registration form has specific columns for the names and signatures of the witnesses.
        
    - **Court Practice:** If a couple elopes and claims they are married but have no witnesses, the court will declare the marriage **Irregular (_Fasid_)**. If they have children, the court will uphold the legitimacy of the children (because Fasid marriages establish paternity), but the wife may not inherit if the husband dies before they "fix" the marriage.
        
- **The "Females Only" Trap:** In exams, if a problem says _"Marriage witnessed by 4 women and 0 men,"_ under Hanafi law, this is **Irregular**. There _must_ be at least one man.
    
- **Exam Comparison Table:**
    
    - **Sunni:** Witnesses essential for Marriage (Irregular without them). No witnesses needed for Divorce.
        
    - **Shia:** No witnesses needed for Marriage (Valid without them). Witnesses essential for Divorce.
