## 9.4 Statutory Procedure of Divorce ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (5/5):** This is the **most important procedural law** for divorce in Bangladesh. It overrides the traditional Sharia method. If you practice law, you will use this Section 7 of the MFLO daily.
    
- **Exam (5/5):** A 100% guaranteed topic. You will face problems like _"Husband pronounced Talaq but didn't send notice"_ or _"Discuss the procedure of Talaq under MFLO 1961."_
    

---

###  Section 7 of MFLO 1961

In Bangladesh, the procedure for effecting a divorce is strictly governed by **Section 7 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961**. This statute modified the traditional law to prevent hasty and arbitrary divorces.

#### Step 1: Pronouncement

The husband must pronounce _Talaq_ in any form recognized by Muslim Law (e.g., Ahsan, Hasan, or even Bida'at/Triple Talaq). The statute does not invalidate the _pronouncement_ itself, but it suspends its effect.

#### Step 2: The Written Notice (Mandatory)

- **To the Chairman:** Immediately after the pronouncement, the husband **MUST** give a written notice of his having done so to the **Chairman of the Arbitration Council** (Union Parishad Chairman or Mayor).
    
- **To the Wife:** He must also supply a copy of the notice to the **Wife**.
    
- **Penalty:** Whoever contravenes this (fails to give notice) shall be punishable with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to **one year** or with a fine which may extend to **10,000 Taka**, or with both.
    

#### Step 3: Formation of Arbitration Council

- Upon receiving the notice, the Chairman must constitute an **Arbitration Council**.
    
- **Composition:** The Chairman + One Representative of the Husband + One Representative of the Wife.
    
- **Duty:** The Council acts as a mediator. Its primary objective is to attempt **reconciliation** between the parties to save the marriage.
    

#### Step 4: The 90-Day Waiting Period (Suspension)

- **The Rule:** A _Talaq_ shall **NOT** be effective until the expiration of **90 days** from the day on which the notice is delivered to the Chairman.
    
- **During this period:**
    
    - The marriage is legally subsisting.
        
    - The husband can **revoke** the divorce at any time (either expressly or by resuming cohabitation).
        
    - If the husband dies during these 90 days, the wife **inherits** from him.
        

#### Step 5: Effectiveness

- **If Reconciliation Fails:** If the 90 days pass and no reconciliation is achieved (and the husband hasn't revoked it), the divorce becomes **effective** and legally binding on the 91st day.
    
- **Pregnant Wife Exception:** If the wife is pregnant at the time of the pronouncement, the divorce does not become effective until the **90 days have passed OR the pregnancy ends**, whichever is **later**.
    

---

### Critical Legal Consequence: "No Notice, No Divorce"

This is the most heavily tested principle in Bangladeshi courts.

**The Rule:** The service of notice to the Chairman is **mandatory**, not directory.

- **Case Law Authority:** _Syed Ali Nawaz Gardezi v. Lt. Col. Yusuf (1963)_.
    
- **The Principle:** If the husband pronounces _Talaq_ but fails to give notice to the Chairman, the _Talaq_ is deemed to have been **revoked** or never effected. In the eyes of the law, the marriage continues as if nothing happened.
    
    - _Scenario:_ A husband pronounces "Triple Talaq" in anger but never sends the letter to the Chairman. 5 years later, he dies.
        
    - _Result:_ The wife is still his legal wife and inherits his property. The divorce never took place legally.
        

### Summary of Section 7 Requirements

|**Action**|**Requirement**|
|---|---|
|**Notice**|**Mandatory**. Without it, divorce is void.|
|**Arbitration**|**Directory**. If the Council fails to meet or reconcile, the divorce _still_ becomes effective after 90 days. (The Chairman cannot "stop" the divorce, only delay it).|
|**Effectiveness**|Suspended for **90 Days**.|
|**Revocability**|Revocable anytime within the 90 days.|

### Important Exam Tip

"Constructive Notice"

If the Chairman is absent or the office is vacant, notice must be served to the person performing the functions of the Chairman. You cannot use "Chairman was unavailable" as an excuse for not sending the notice.
