### 3.2 Books of Tafsir ⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (⭐ 4/5):** While the Quran is the primary source of law, the raw text often contains general principles. To understand the _application_ of these principles (especially in family law), one must understand how these verses have been interpreted historically.
    
- **Exam (⭐ 3/5):** Less frequently asked as a standalone descriptive question compared to "Sources of Law" or "Marriage." However, it is vital for understanding the background of specific statutes and is common in viva voce (oral exams).
    

---

#### Introduction: Unlocking the Legislative Text

**Tafsir** refers to the science of explanation, interpretation, and commentary on the Quran. It comes from the root word _fassara_, meaning "to explain" or "to expound."

As a source of information for Muslim Family Law, Books of Tafsir are crucial because the Quran is not arranged like a modern legal code. It does not list "Section 1: Marriage, Section 2: Divorce." Instead, legal injunctions (_Ahkam_) are scattered throughout the text. Books of Tafsir organize, clarify, and contextualize these verses, allowing jurists to derive specific legal rulings.

#### The Legal Verses (Ayat al-Ahkam)

A student of law does not study the Tafsir of the entire Quran. Instead, the focus is on **Ayat al-Ahkam** (Verses of Ordinances/Judgments).

- Scholars estimate that out of over 6,000 verses, only about **80 to 500 verses** deal strictly with legal commands.
    
- The vast majority of these legal verses pertain to **Family Law** (Marriage, Divorce, Inheritance, Dower).
    
- Therefore, Books of Tafsir are the primary information source for the "why" and "how" of family law regulations.
    

#### Categories of Tafsir

To evaluate the reliability of a source, one must know its methodology:

**1. Tafsir bi-al-Riwaya (Tafsir by Tradition)** This is the interpretation of the Quran using:

- Other parts of the Quran.
    
- The Hadith of the Prophet.
    
- The reports of the Sahaba (Companions).
    
- _Relevance:_ This is considered the most authoritative form of interpretation for establishing fixed laws.
    
- _Example:_ _Tafsir Ibn Kathir_.
    

**2. Tafsir bi-al-Diraya (Tafsir by Opinion/Reason)** This involves interpretation based on reason, logic, and linguistic analysis, provided it does not contradict the fundamental principles of Islam.

- _Relevance:_ This allows for the adaptability of law (Ijtihad) to modern contexts, which is relevant when discussing statutory reforms in Bangladesh.
    
- _Example:_ _Al-Kashshaf_ by Al-Zamakhshari.
    

#### Key Tafsir Works for Legal Study

For a student of Muslim Law (specifically Hanafi law in Bangladesh), the following works are most cited regarding legal deduction:

**1. Ahkam al-Quran by Al-Jassas (d. 370 AH)**

- **Significance:** This is a specialized **Hanafi** Tafsir. It focuses strictly on the legal verses. Since Bangladesh follows the Hanafi school, this book is a primary reference for understanding the classical Hanafi interpretation of Quranic verses regarding marriage and divorce.
    

**2. Al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Quran by Al-Qurtubi (d. 671 AH)**

- **Significance:** Widely regarded as the best "Legal Tafsir." Although the author was Maliki, his work is encyclopedic and discusses the legal opinions of all schools impartially. It provides deep legal analysis of every verse that contains a rule.
    

**3. Tafsir al-Kabir by Fakhr al-Din al-Razi**

- **Significance:** Focuses on the theological and rational aspects of the law. It is often used to understand the "wisdom" (_Hikmah_) behind a law (e.g., why is inheritance divided 2:1 between male and female?).
    

#### Use in Modern Legal Systems

In the courts of Bangladesh, judges rarely interpret the Quran directly (due to the doctrine of _Taqleed_ or following established schools). However, when the High Court Division or Appellate Division faces a new issue not covered by standard Fiqh books (or when interpreting the _Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961_ against Sharia compliance), they may refer to these Books of Tafsir to determine the original intent of the Quranic command.

### Important Notes & Tips

> - **Concept to Master:** **Asbab al-Nuzul (Occasions of Revelation)**. This is a critical component found in Tafsir books. It explains the specific historical event that caused a verse to be revealed. For example, the verses on _Zihar_ (an archaic form of divorce) were revealed regarding a specific complaint by a woman named Khawla bint Tha'labah. Knowing the "Asbab" limits the law to its proper context.
>     
> - **Differentiation:** Do not confuse _Tafsir_ (Interpretation of Quran) with _Ta'wil_ (Allegorical interpretation). In legal contexts, _Tafsir_ is the standard term.
>     
> - **Tip:** If asked about the "Verses of Inheritance" (Surah An-Nisa, verses 11, 12, and 176), mention that **Al-Jassas** provides the specific Hanafi arithmetic calculations derived from these verses.
