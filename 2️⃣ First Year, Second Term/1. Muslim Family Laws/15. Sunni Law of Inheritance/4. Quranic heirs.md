
## 📌 15.4 Quranic Heirs (_Zavil-Furuz_) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

>**Rating Justification:**
>- **Understanding (5/5):** These heirs are the foundation of the entire Sunni inheritance system. Their rights are divine mandates (fixed in the Quran), meaning no human law or will can override them.
>- **Exam (5/5):** You will be asked to list the "12 Quranic Heirs" or determine the specific share of a Mother, Husband, or Daughter in a problem question.

The **Quranic Heirs**, technically known as **Sharers** (_Zavil-Furuz_), are the first class of heirs in Sunni Law. They are called "Quranic" because their specific fractional shares are expressly ordained in the Holy Quran (primarily in Surah An-Nisa).

- **Priority:** They take their shares **first**, immediately after the payment of funeral expenses, debts, and wills.
    
- **The Fixed Fractions:** The shares assigned to them are limited to six specific fractions: **1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 2/3, 1/3, and 1/6**.
    

### 🔷 The List of 12 Heirs 
There are exactly 12 relations who fall into this category. Of these, **4 are Males** and **8 are Females**. This highlights how Islam introduced inheritance rights for women who were previously excluded under pre-Islamic custom.

They can be grouped into four categories for easier memorization:

#### Group A: Heirs by Marriage (Affinity)

These two never lose their status as heirs.

1. **Husband:** Gets **1/4** (if wife has children) or **1/2** (if no children).
    
2. **Wife:** Gets **1/8** (if husband has children) or **1/4** (if no children).
    

#### Group B: Ascendants (Parents & Grandparents)

3. **Father:** Gets **1/6** (if there is a son). Can also be a Residuary.
    
4. **True Grandfather:** Gets **1/6** (only if Father is dead).
    
5. **Mother:** Gets **1/6** (if there is a child/siblings) or **1/3** (if no child/siblings).
    
6. **True Grandmother:** Gets **1/6** (if no Mother exists).
    

#### Group C: Descendants (Females only)
- _Note: The Son is NOT a Quranic heir; he is a Residuary._ 
7. **Daughter:** Gets **1/2** (if alone) or **2/3** (if two or more). Becomes Residuary with Son.
8. **Son's Daughter:** Takes the Daughter's place if she is absent. Gets **1/6** if there is one direct Daughter.

#### Group D: Collaterals (Siblings)

9. **Full Sister:** Gets **1/2** (if alone) or **2/3** (if two or more).
    
10. **Consanguine Sister:** Gets **1/2** or **2/3** (if no Full Sister). Gets **1/6** if there is one Full Sister.
    
11. **Uterine Brother:** Gets **1/6** (if alone) or **1/3** shared equally (if two or more).
    
12. **Uterine Sister:** Same shares as Uterine Brother.
    

---

### 🔷 The "Primary Heirs" (The Un-Excludables)

Among the Quranic heirs and Residuaries, there are **Five Persons** who are called the Primary Heirs. They are **never totally excluded** from inheritance by any other heir (though their shares may reduce).

1. **Husband**
    
2. **Wife**
    
3. **Father**
    
4. **Mother**
    
5. **Daughter** _(Note: The Son is also a primary heir, but he is a Residuary, not a Quranic Heir)._
    

### 🔷 Anomalous Rule: The "Converting" Heirs

Four of the female Quranic heirs lose their "Fixed Share" status and convert into "Residuaries" if a male of the same degree exists. This is to ensure the male gets double the female's portion in the residue.

- **Daughter** (converts with Son).
    
- **Son's Daughter** (converts with Son's Son).
    
- **Full Sister** (converts with Full Brother).
    
- **Consanguine Sister** (converts with Consanguine Brother).
    

_Crucial Distinction:_ The **Uterine Sister** does NOT convert. She shares equally (1:1) with the Uterine Brother, and they remain Quranic Heirs together.

---

### 🔷 Important Notes & Tips for Exams

- **The Number 4:** There are **4 Male** Quranic heirs: Father, Husband, True Grandfather, Uterine Brother. All other males (Son, Brother, Uncle) are Residuaries.
    
- **The "Girl Power" Fraction:** The fraction **2/3** is exclusively for females. It is given when there are **two or more** Daughters, Son's Daughters, or Sisters. No male ever gets a fixed 2/3 share.
    
- **Father vs. Husband:** Do not confuse their shares.
    
    - Husband = 1/2 or 1/4.
        
    - Father = 1/6 (plus residue).
        
- **Uterine Unique Rule:** Uterine siblings are the _only_ Quranic heirs where males and females inherit **equal shares** (1/6 each or 1/3 shared equally).
