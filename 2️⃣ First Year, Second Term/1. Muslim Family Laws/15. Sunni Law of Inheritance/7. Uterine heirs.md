## 15.7 Uterine Heirs (Distant Kindred) ⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (3/5):** This is the "Third Class" of heirs. They represent the female side of the family (cognates). It is conceptually important to know they exist, even if they rarely get money.
    
- **Exam (3/5):** Problems involving Distant Kindred are rare because the calculations are extremely complex. You are more likely to face a theoretical question: _"Who are Distant Kindred and when do they inherit?"_
    

---

### 1. Definition: Who are "Uterine Heirs"?

In the context of the syllabus sequence (after Sharers and Agnates), "Uterine Heirs" refers to **Class III: Distant Kindred** (_Zavil-Arham_).

- **Definition:** These are blood relations who are **neither** Sharers (Quranic Heirs) **nor** Residuaries (Agnates).
    
- **Why "Uterine"?** Because they are generally related to the deceased through a **female** (the uterus/womb).
    
- **Examples:**
    
    - Daughter's children (related through daughter).
        
    - Sister's children (related through sister).
        
    - Maternal Uncles and Aunts (related through mother).
        
    - Paternal Aunts (Father's sisters).
        

### 2. The Rule of Inheritance (When do they get paid?)

They are the "Waitlisted" heirs. They inherit **ONLY** if two conditions are met:

1. **No Sharers exist** (except Husband/Wife).
    
2. **No Residuaries exist**.
    

**The Golden Rule:** If there is even **one** Agnate (e.g., a remote cousin or nephew), the Distant Kindred get **ZERO**.

- _Scenario:_ Deceased leaves a **Daughter's Son** (Distant Kindred) and a **Brother's Son** (Residuary).
    
- _Result:_ Brother's Son takes everything. Daughter's Son gets nothing.
    

---

### 3. Classification (The 4 Groups)

Similar to Residuaries, Distant Kindred are divided into four classes based on priority. Class 1 excludes Class 2, and so on.

#### Class I: Descendants

- Children of daughters.
    
- Children of son's daughters.
    
- _Example:_ **Your Daughter's Son** or Daughter's Daughter.
    

#### Class II: Ascendants

- False Grandparents (Ancestors not in the direct male line).
    
- _Example:_ **Mother's Father** (Maternal Grandfather).
    

#### Class III: Descendants of Parents

- Descendants of brothers and sisters (who are not Residuaries).
    
- _Example:_ **Sister's Son/Daughter**, Brother's Daughter.
    

#### Class IV: Descendants of Grandparents

- Collaterals who are not Residuaries.
    
- _Example:_ **Paternal Aunts** (Father's sisters), **Maternal Uncles and Aunts** (Mother's siblings).
    

---

### 4. Critical Distinction: Uterine Brother vs. Uterine Heir

**This is the #1 Exam Trap.** Do not confuse these two terms!

|**Term**|**Identity**|**Class**|**Priority**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Uterine Brother**|Your brother from the same mother but different father.|**Sharer** (Class I)|**High.** Gets fixed 1/6.|
|**Uterine Heir** (_Zavil-Arham_)|Relations through females (e.g., Maternal Uncle, Daughter's Son).|**Distant Kindred** (Class III)|**Low.** Inherits only if no one else exists.|

### Summary of the Entire Inheritance System

To wrap up Topic 15, here is the "Master Algorithm" for solving any Sunni inheritance problem:

1. **Pay Debts:** Funeral and debts first.
    
2. **Assign Shares (Class I):** Give the Wife, Mother, Father, Daughter, etc., their fixed fractions (1/2, 1/6, etc.).
    
3. **Check for Residue (Class II):** Is there money left? Give it to the nearest Agnate (Son, Brother, Uncle).
    
    - _If Shares > 1:_ Apply **'Awl**.
        
    - _If Shares < 1 and No Residuary:_ Apply **Radd**.
        
4. **Check Class III:** Only if the chart is empty of blood relations (Sharers/Residuaries), look for Distant Kindred.
