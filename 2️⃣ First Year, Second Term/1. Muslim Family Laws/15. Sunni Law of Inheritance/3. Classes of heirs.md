## 📌 15.3 Classes of Heirs (Sunni Law) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

>**Rating Justification:**
>- **Understanding (5/5):** This is the absolute core of the inheritance system. Without memorizing these classes and their specific conditions, you cannot solve any distribution problem.
>- **Exam (5/5):** Every inheritance question requires you to identify the heirs first. You must know exactly who falls into which category to determine who gets a fixed share and who gets the residue.


Sunni Law divides heirs into three distinct classes. The distribution follows a strict **Order of Priority**. You must exhaust the funds in Class I before giving anything to Class II, and Class III only gets property if Class I and II are effectively empty (except for the spouse).

1. **Class I: The Sharers (_Zavil-Furuz_):** They take their fixed Quranic shares first.
    
2. **Class II: The Residuaries (_Asaba_):** They take the "Residue" (whatever is left over).
    
3. **Class III: Distant Kindred (_Zavil-Arham_):** They inherit **only** if there are no Residuaries and no Sharers (other than the Husband/Wife).
    

---

### CLASS I: The Sharers (_Zavil-Furuz_)

These are the 12 heirs (4 Males, 8 Females) whose shares are specifically fixed in the Quran. They are the "VIPs" of inheritance.

#### A. The Spouses (Always Inherit)

They are never excluded by anyone.
1. **Husband:**
    
    - **1/4:** If the wife has children (or son's children, how low soever).
        
    - **1/2:** If the wife has **NO** children (or son's children).
        
2. **Wife:**
    
    - **1/8:** If the husband has children (or son's children).
        
    - **1/4:** If the husband has **NO** children.
        
    - _Note:_ If there are multiple wives (up to 4), they collectively share this 1/8 or 1/4. It is not per wife.
        

#### B. The Parents & Grandparents

3. **Father:**
    
    - **1/6:** If there is a Son or Son's Son (Male descendant).
        
    - **1/6 + Residue:** If there are only Daughters/Son's Daughters (Female descendants).
        
    - _(As Pure Residuary):_ If there are no children at all.
        
4. **Mother:**
    
    - **1/6:** If there is a child, OR a son's child, OR **two or more siblings** (brothers/sisters) of the deceased.
        
    - **1/3:** If there is no child, no son's child, and not more than one sibling.
        
    - **1/3 of Residue:** In the special "Umariyyatain" cases (Husband+Mother+Father OR Wife+Mother+Father).
        
5. **True Grandfather (Father's Father):**
    
    - Inherits **only** if the Father is dead.
        
    - Gets the exact same shares as the Father (1/6, etc.).
        
    - _Excluded by:_ Father.
        
6. **True Grandmother:**
    
    - **1/6:** Shared equally between Maternal (Mother's Mother) and Paternal (Father's Mother) grandmothers if both exist.
        
    - _Excluded by:_ Mother (always). Paternal Grandmother is also excluded by the Father.
        

#### C. The Descendants (Females)

7. **Daughter:**
    
    - **1/2:** If she is the **only** one.
        
    - **2/3:** If there are **two or more** (shared equally).
        
    - _Status Change:_ If a **Son** exists, she becomes a **Residuary** with him (2:1 ratio).
        
8. **Son's Daughter:**
    
    - **1/2:** If one (and no direct daughter exists).
        
    - **2/3:** If two or more (and no direct daughter exists).
        
    - **1/6:** If there is exactly **one direct Daughter**. (Logic: Daughter gets 1/2 + Son's Daughter gets 1/6 = 2/3 total reserved for "girls").
        
    - _Excluded by:_ Son, or **two or more** direct Daughters.
        
    - _Status Change:_ Becomes Residuary with Son's Son.
        

#### D. The Collaterals (Siblings)

9. **Full Sister:**
    
    - **1/2:** If one.
        
    - **2/3:** If two or more.
        
    - _Excluded by:_ Son, Son's Son, Father, True Grandfather.
        
    - _Status Change:_ Becomes Residuary with Full Brother.
        
10. **Consanguine Sister (Half-sister by Father):**
    
    - **1/2 or 2/3:** Only if no Full Sisters exist.
        
    - **1/6:** If there is exactly **one Full Sister**. (To make up the 2/3 total).
        
    - _Excluded by:_ Full Brother, or **two or more** Full Sisters.
        
11. **Uterine Brother** & 12. **Uterine Sister (Half-siblings by Mother):**
    
    - **1/6:** If one (Male or Female).
        
    - **1/3:** If two or more.
        
    - _Unique Rule:_ They share equally (Male = Female). The 2:1 rule does NOT apply to them.
        
    - _Excluded by:_ Any Child, Son's Child, Father, True Grandfather.

### CLASS II: The Residuaries (_Asaba_)

These heirs receive the "Residue" (leftover estate) after the Sharers are paid. If there are no Sharers, they take the whole estate. They are classified into 4 Groups.

**Rule of Priority:** Group 1 excludes Group 2. Group 2 excludes Group 3, and so on.

#### Group 1: Descendants (Male)

1. **Son:** The highest heir. Excludes all siblings and uncles.
    
2. **Son's Son:** Inherits only if Son is dead.
    

#### Group 2: Ascendants (Male)

3. **Father:** Inherits as residuary if no children.
    
4. **True Grandfather:** Inherits if Father is dead.
    

#### Group 3: Descendants of Father (Siblings)

5. **Full Brother.**
    
6. **Consanguine Brother** (Father's side).
    
7. **Full Brother's Son** (Nephew).
    
8. **Consanguine Brother's Son.**
    

#### Group 4: Descendants of Grandfather (Uncles)

9. **Full Paternal Uncle.**
    
10. **Consanguine Paternal Uncle.**
    
11. **Full Paternal Uncle's Son** (Cousin).
    
12. **Consanguine Paternal Uncle's Son.**
    

**The "Residuary by Another" (_Asaba bil-ghayr_)** This is a crucial mechanism. Four specific females (who are normally Sharers) are "dragged" into the Residuary class if a male of the same degree exists. They share the residue in a **2:1 ratio (Male:Female)**.

1. **Daughter** (with Son).
    
2. **Son's Daughter** (with Son's Son).
    
3. **Full Sister** (with Full Brother).
    
4. **Consanguine Sister** (with Consanguine Brother).
    

_Note:_ Uterine sisters never become residuaries.

---

### CLASS III: Distant Kindred (_Zavil-Arham_)

These are blood relations who are **neither Sharers nor Residuaries**. They are mostly female agnates or relations through the mother. They inherit **ONLY** if Class I and Class II are completely empty (except for Husband/Wife).

They are categorized into 4 Classes (similar to Residuaries but for the "excluded" ones):

1. **Descendants:**
    
    - Daughter's children (e.g., Daughter's Son).
        
    - Son's Daughter's children.
        
2. **Ascendants:**
    
    - False Grandfather (Mother's Father).
        
    - False Grandmother (Mother's Father's Mother).
        
3. **Descendants of Parents:**
    
    - Sister's children (Nephews/Nieces through sister).
        
    - Brother's daughters.
        
4. **Descendants of Grandparents:**
    
    - Paternal Aunts (Father's sisters).
        
    - Maternal Uncles and Aunts.
        

---

### Important "Blocking" Rules for Exams

1. **The Son Block:** If a **Son** is present, ALL brothers, sisters, uncles, and aunts get **ZERO**.
    
2. **The Father Block:** If a **Father** is present, ALL brothers, sisters, and grandparents get **ZERO**.
    
3. **The Brother Block:** A Full Brother blocks a Consanguine Brother (and all uncles).
---

### 🔷 Important Notes & Tips for Exams

- **The "Zero" Trap:** In an exam, if you see a "Daughter's Son" listed alongside a "Paternal Uncle," give the Daughter's Son **Zero**.
    
    - _Reason:_ Paternal Uncle is Class II (Residuary). Daughter's Son is Class III (Distant Kindred). Class II blocks Class III.
        
- **The "Sister" Anomaly:** A Full Sister usually gets 1/2. But if she is with a **Daughter**, the Sister becomes a Residuary (_Asaba ma'a ghayriha_).
    
    - _Example:_ Daughter (1/2) + Sister (Residue 1/2). This is a unique exception where a female becomes a residuary with another female.
        
- **Primary Heirs Never Excluded:** Five heirs are "Primary" and can never be totally excluded: **Husband, Wife, Mother, Father, Daughter.** (The Son is also never excluded but is a Residuary, not a Sharer).
