## 7.3 Types of Dower ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (⭐ 5/5):** A lawyer cannot draft a marriage deed or file a suit for dower without understanding these categories. The difference between "Prompt" and "Deferred" determines _when_ a wife can sue and _when_ the limitation period begins.
    
- **Exam (⭐ 5/5):** This is the most common short-note topic. Expect questions like: _"Distinguish between Mu'ajjal and Mu'wajjal dower"_ or _"How is Mahr-e-Mithl determined?"_
    

---

### Classification of Dower

Dower is classified in two ways: first, based on **how it is fixed**, and second, based on **when it is paid**.

### A. Classification by Mode of Fixation

This classification asks: _Was the amount written in the contract or not?_

#### 1. Specified Dower (_Mahr-e-Musamma_)

- **Definition:** The dower which is expressly agreed upon by the husband and wife at the time of marriage (or even after marriage).
    
- **Form:** It is usually specified in **Column 13** of the Bangladeshi _Kabinnama_.
    
- **Authority:** The court must enforce this exact amount, provided it is not below the legal minimum (10 Dirhams).
    

#### 2. Proper / Customary Dower (_Mahr-e-Mithl_)

- **Definition:** The dower that is implied by law when the marriage contract is **silent** regarding dower, or when the specified amount is void (e.g., "I will give you a pig").
    
- **Concept:** It is the "Market Value" of the bride.
    
- **Factors for Calculation:** It is not based on the husband's wealth, but on the dower paid to other women in the **wife's father's family** (_Agnates_) who are similar to her in:
    
    1. **Lineage:** Her father's sisters (aunts) or her own sisters. (Not her mother, unless the mother is a cousin from the father's side).
        
    2. **Attributes:** Beauty, Age, Virginity, Education, and Character.
        
    3. **Time:** The economic conditions of the time.
        

---

### B. Classification by Time of Payment

This classification asks: _When is the money due?_ This is legally more significant for enforcement.

#### 1. Prompt Dower (_Mu'ajjal_)

- **Literal Meaning:** "That which is hastened."
    
- **Definition:** The portion of the dower which is payable **immediately** upon demand by the wife.
    
- **Legal Consequence:**
    
    - **Right of Refusal:** Until the Prompt Dower is paid, the wife has the absolute right to **refuse to live with the husband** or admit him to sexual intercourse.
        
    - **Limitation Period:** The 3-year limitation period for suing begins from the date she demands it and he refuses.
        

#### 2. Deferred Dower (_Mu'wajjal_)

- **Literal Meaning:** "That which is delayed."
    
- **Definition:** The portion of the dower which is payable only upon the **dissolution** of the marriage.
    
- **When is it Due?** It becomes due on:
    
    1. **Divorce** (_Talaq_).
        
    2. **Death** of either party.
        
- **Purpose:** It acts as a financial security for the wife and a deterrent for the husband against arbitrary divorce (because he knows he will have to pay a lump sum instantly).
    
- **Limitation Period:** The 3-year limitation period begins from the date of dissolution (Death/Divorce).
    

---

### C. The Rule of Presumption (Ambiguity)

What if the _Kabinnama_ says "Dower is 10 Lakh Taka" but does not specify how much is Prompt and how much is Deferred?

- **Sunni / Hanafi Law:**
    
    - Part is Prompt, Part is Deferred. The proportion is regulated by **Custom** (_Urf_) or the status of the parties.
        
    - _Bangladeshi Court Practice:_ In absence of custom, courts often treat the **whole amount as Prompt** to favor the wife, allowing her to demand it anytime.
        
- **Shia Law:** The presumption is that the **whole dower is Prompt**.
    

---

### 📝 Important Notes & Tips

- **Spelling Trap:** Be very careful with the spelling.
    
    - **Mu'ajjal** (with an 'a') = Prompt (Hurry).
        
    - **Mu'wajjal** (with a 'w') = Deferred (Wait).
        
    - _Mnemonic:_ **A** for **At once** (Prompt). **W** for **Wait** (Deferred).
        
- **Exam Scenario:** _"A wife demands her dower while the marriage is intact. The husband says 'I will pay when we divorce.' Is he right?"_
    
    - **Answer:** It depends. If it is **Prompt**, he is wrong; he must pay now. If it is **Deferred**, he is right; she must wait for dissolution. If unspecified, the Court decides based on custom (usually favoring Prompt).
        
- **Real-World Tip:** In Bangladesh, usually column 14 of the Kabinnama specifies the split (e.g., "Half Prompt, Half Deferred"). Lawyers should check this before advising a wife to leave her husband for non-payment.
