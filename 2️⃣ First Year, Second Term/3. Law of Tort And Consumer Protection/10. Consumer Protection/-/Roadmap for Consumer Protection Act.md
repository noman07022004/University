## ⚖️ 2. Consumer Protection Laws in Bangladesh

### 💠 The Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009 (Bangladesh)

Learn the **main sections** and their **objectives**:

- **Definition Section (Section 2):** Important terms like _consumer, goods, service, unfair practice, defect, etc._
    
- **Rights of Consumers (Section 3):**
    
    - Right to safety
        
    - Right to information
        
    - Right to choose
        
    - Right to be heard
        
    - Right to redress
        
- **Prohibitions and Offences:**
    
    - Adulteration of food or goods
        
    - False or misleading advertisements
        
    - Not showing price list
        
    - Selling expired or fake goods
        
    - Black marketing or hoarding
        
- **Punishments and Penalties:**
    
    - Fines and imprisonment (know the common examples — like fine for price list violation or false labeling)
        
- **Authority and Enforcement:**
    
    - Directorate of National Consumer Rights Protection (DNCRP)
        
    - Powers and functions of DNCRP officers
        
    - Procedure for filing complaints
        
    - Right to compensation for consumers
