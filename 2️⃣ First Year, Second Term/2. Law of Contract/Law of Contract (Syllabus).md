## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Explain and analyze the nature of a contract and its use and value in society;
2. Determine the elements of a void contract;
3. Find out contractual defects, methods of discharging and breaching a contract and remedies for breach of contract;
4. Recognize the formal requirements necessary to create a valid contract;

### **Strategic Summary**

Your highest priority "gap" areas are:

1. **Agency:** Rights/Duties of Principal/Agent (9.11) and Agent's Authority (9.6).
    
2. **Bailment:** Rights/Duties of the _Bailee_ (8.4) and Finder of Goods (8.6).
    
3. **Indemnity:** Rights/Liabilities of the _Surety_ (7.5, 7.6).
    
4. **Formation:** The fundamentals: Offer (2.1), Acceptance (2.2), and Consideration (2.4).
    

Topics to spend the _least_ time on (as they were asked in 2024):

- Quasi-Contracts (Topic 3)
    
- Remedies (Topic 6)
    
- Doctrine of Frustration (5.8)
    
- Definition of Pledge (8.8)
