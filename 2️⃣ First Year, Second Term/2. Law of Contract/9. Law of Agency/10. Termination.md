## 9.10: Termination (of Agency) (Overall: ⭐⭐⭐⭐)

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (⭐⭐⭐⭐):** Very important. This subtopic explains the "end" of the agency lifecycle. It is crucial to know how an agent's authority is extinguished, as this determines when the principal is no longer bound by the agent's acts.
    
- **Exam (⭐⭐⭐⭐):** High importance. This is a very common topic for short notes (e.g., "How can an agency be terminated?") and a critical part of problem questions (e.g., "P died. Can A still sell the house? What is an irrevocable agency?").


**Termination of agency** is the end of the legal relationship between the principal and the agent. When an agency is terminated, the agent's authority to act on behalf of the principal is extinguished.

The **Contract Act, 1872,** provides a complete list of the ways an agency can be terminated in **Section 201**. These methods are broadly divided into two categories: termination by the _act of the parties_ and termination by _operation of law_.

### 1. Methods of Termination (Section 201)

**A) By the Act of the Parties:**

1. **Revocation by the Principal:** The principal may **revoke** (cancel) the agent's authority at any time before the authority has been exercised so as to bind the principal (**Section 203**).
    
2. **Renunciation by the Agent:** The agent may **renounce** (quit) the business of the agency.
    

- **Rules for this:** If the agency is for a fixed period, the party terminating early _without_ just cause must **compensate** the other for the loss (**Section 205**). **Reasonable notice** must also be given (**Section 206**).
    

**B) By Operation of Law:**

An agency terminates _automatically_, by law, in the following situations:

1. **Completion of the Business:** When the purpose of the agency is finished (e.g., the agent was hired to sell a house, and the house is sold).
    
2. **Death or Insanity:** When _either_ the principal _or_ the agent dies or becomes of unsound mind.
    
3. **Insolvency of the Principal:** When the principal is "adjudicated an insolvent."
    

---

### The Key Exception: Irrevocable Agency (Section 202)

This is the most important exception to the rules of termination, and it is a critical, integrated concept.

- **The Rule:** "Where the agent has himself an **interest in the property** which forms the subject-matter of the agency, the agency **cannot**, in the absence of an express contract, **be terminated** to the prejudice of such interest."
    
- **What this means:** This is an **"agency coupled with an interest."** It is a situation where the agency is created _not_ just for the principal's benefit, but also to provide a **security** for the agent.
    
- **Consequence:** An agency coupled with an interest **CANNOT be terminated** by the principal's revocation, insanity, or death.
    
- **Classic Example (from Act, Illus. (a) to S. 202):**
    
    - A (Principal) borrows 5,000 Taka from B (Agent).
        
    - As security for the loan, A gives B the authority to sell A's land and to pay himself (B) the 5,000 Taka out of the sale proceeds.
        
    - This is an **irrevocable agency**. A _cannot_ revoke this authority, and it will _not_ be terminated even if A dies or becomes insane. B can still sell the land to get his money back.
        

---

### When Does Termination Take Effect? (Section 208)

This is a crucial rule for protecting third parties.

- **The Rule:** The termination of an agent's authority is _not_ effective immediately.
    
    - **As against the Agent:** It takes effect only when it becomes **known to the agent**.
        
    - **As against Third Parties:** It takes effect only when it becomes **known to the third parties**.
        
- **Example:**
    
    - P (Principal) fires A (his agent) by sending him a letter.
        
    - _Before_ the letter arrives, A (who doesn't know he's been fired) enters into a contract with T (a third party who has dealt with A before).
        
    - **Result:** P is **bound** by this contract. The termination was not effective against A (who didn't know) or T (who also didn't know).
        

---

### 🔔 Important Notes & Tips

- **Master the S. 201 List:** For any exam question, you must know the list from Section 201 (Revocation, Renunciation, Completion, Death, Insanity, Insolvency).
    
- **The S. 202 Exception is Key:** The most common "trick" question involves "agency coupled with an interest." You must be able to identify it (it's a security for the agent) and know that it is _irrevocable_.
    
- **The S. 208 Rule (Knowledge):** Termination is only effective when it is _communicated_. A principal who fires their agent must immediately inform all third parties who deal with that agent; otherwise, the principal will _still_ be bound by the agent's "apparent authority."
