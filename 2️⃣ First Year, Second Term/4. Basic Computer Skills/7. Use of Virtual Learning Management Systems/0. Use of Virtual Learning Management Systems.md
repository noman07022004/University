## 7. Use of Virtual Learning Management Systems ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (4/5):** Essential for your daily academic life. You will likely submit 90% of your assignments and receive all official course notices through this platform.
    
- **Exam (3/5):** Moderate Frequency. Exams may ask for the "Features of an LMS," "Advantages of E-learning," or practical steps like "How to join a class or submit an assignment in Google Classroom."
    

---

### The Digital Classroom

A **Learning Management System (LMS)** is a software application designed to handle the administration, documentation, tracking, reporting, and delivery of educational courses. It is the "virtual building" of your university. Just as you go to a physical room for a lecture, you log into an LMS to find materials, submit work, and check grades.

While systems like Moodle or Blackboard are common globally, the syllabus focuses on **Google Classroom**, which has become the standard in Bangladesh due to its simplicity and integration with the Google ecosystem (Gmail, Drive, Docs).

#### Google Classroom: The Academic Hub

Google Classroom is a free web service developed by Google for schools that aims to simplify creating, distributing, and grading assignments. It essentially eliminates the need for paper in the assignment workflow.

- **The Ecosystem Integration:** Google Classroom does not work in isolation. It acts as a "Command Center" that connects other Google services:
    
    - **Google Drive:** Every time you join a class, a folder is automatically created in your Drive to store your files.
        
    - **Google Docs/Slides:** Used for writing the actual assignments.
        
    - **Google Calendar:** Automatically updates with due dates for your assignments so you never miss a deadline.
        
    - **Gmail:** Sends you notifications when a teacher posts a new material or returns your grade.
        
- **Bangladeshi Context:**
    
    - Before 2020, LMS use in Bangladesh was rare. The COVID-19 pandemic forced a massive digital transformation.
        
    - Now, even for physical classes at Khulna University, teachers often prefer receiving assignments via Classroom to save paper and ensure a digital timestamp (proof of submission).

### Practical Components: Interface & Workflow

The Google Classroom interface is divided into four specific tabs (for students/teachers):

**1. The Stream (The Notice Board)**

- This is the "Home Page" of a specific course.
    
- **Function:** It displays announcements, upcoming deadlines, and general discussion. It is arranged chronologically (newest on top).
    
- _Tip:_ Do not look for specific assignment files here; they get buried under new posts. Use the "Classwork" tab instead.
    

**2. Classwork (The Library & Desk)**

- This is the most important tab. It organizes content by **Topics** (e.g., "Chapter 1: Basics," "Mid-Term Exam").
    
- **Materials:** Lecture slides, PDFs, or YouTube links posted by the teacher.
    
- **Assignments:** Tasks you must complete. They have a specific status:
    
    - _Assigned:_ You have work to do.
        
    - _Turned In:_ You have submitted it.
        
    - _Graded:_ The teacher has checked it.
        
    - _Missing:_ The deadline has passed, and you did not submit.
        

**3. People**

- Lists the **Teachers** and your **Classmates**. You can email them directly from here.
    

**4. Grades (Student View: "View Your Work")**

- Shows a summary of your scores for that specific class.

### _Additional Necessary Information:_ The Submission Process

_This is the single most critical practical skill. Many new students "upload" the file but forget to "submit" it._

1. **Attachment:** You click "Add or Create" to upload your file (from your laptop or Google Drive).
    
2. **The "Turn In" / "Mark as Done" Button:** Uploading is not enough. You _must_ click **"Turn In."**
    
    - _Analogy:_ Uploading is like writing your homework in your notebook. Clicking "Turn In" is like physically handing the notebook to the teacher. If you don't click it, the teacher sees "Missing."
        
3. **Unsubmit:** If you make a mistake, you can "Unsubmit" to retrieve the paper, fix it, and submit it again (as long as the deadline hasn't passed).

### Important Notes & Exam Tips

- **Private Comments vs. Class Comments:**
    
    - **Class Comments:** Visible to _everyone_. Use this for general questions (e.g., "Is the deadline 5 PM or 11 PM?").
        
    - **Private Comments:** Visible _only to the teacher_. Use this for personal issues (e.g., "I uploaded the wrong file" or "I am sick").
        
- **The "Code" vs. "Invite":**
    
    - You can join a class in two ways: Entering a distinct **Class Code** (e.g., `xyz123`) provided by the teacher, or clicking an **Email Invitation**.
        
- **Exam Question:** "What are the benefits of using an LMS like Google Classroom?"
    
    - _Answer points:_ Paperless (Eco-friendly), Accessibility (Anywhere/Anytime), Organization (Automatic folders), and Immediate Feedback (Grades released instantly).
