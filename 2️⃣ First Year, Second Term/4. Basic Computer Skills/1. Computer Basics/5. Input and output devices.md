### 1.5 Input and Output Devices ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (5/5):** Fundamental. These devices are the only way humans interact with the machine. Without them, the computer is a "black box" that cannot be used.
    
- **Exam (5/5):** High Frequency. Questions like "Differentiate between Impact and Non-Impact printers," "Explain the working of OMR," or "List three input and three output devices" are standard.
    

---

### The Interface

The computer's Central Processing Unit (CPU) operates in binary (machine language) and at blinding speeds. Humans speak natural languages and operate in the physical world. **Input and Output (I/O) devices** act as the translators or bridges between these two vastly different worlds. They are collectively known as **Peripherals**.

#### A. Input Devices (Data Entry)

An Input Device is any hardware component that allows you to send data, programs, or control signals _into_ the computer for processing.

**1. Keyboard**

- **Function:** The primary device for entering text and characters.
    
- **Mechanism:** When a key is pressed, it closes a circuit, sending a specific signal (Scan Code) to the CPU, which converts it into ASCII (e.g., 'A' = 65).
    
- **Layout:** The standard layout is **QWERTY**.
    
- **User Context:** Your **ASUS Strix GL503GE** likely features a specialized **Backlit Chiclet Keyboard**. This is designed for precision, often with "N-Key Rollover" (allowing multiple keys to be pressed at once without jamming—crucial for gaming or fast typing).
    

**2. Pointing Devices**

- **Mouse:** A handheld device that controls a cursor (pointer) on the screen. Modern mice are **Optical** (using light/LED) or **Laser** (higher precision).
    
- **Touchpad:** A flat surface found on laptops (like yours) that translates finger motion into cursor movement.
    
- **Joystick:** Primarily used for flight simulators and gaming to control direction.
    

3. Scanning & Reading Devices (Crucial for Exams/Business)

These are vital for digitizing physical data.

- **Scanner:** Converts hard copy (physical paper) into soft copy (digital image/PDF).
    
- **OMR (Optical Mark Recognition):** Detects marks on paper. **Bangladeshi Context:** This is extremely relevant to you. The answer scripts for your Khulna University admission test and potentially your semester exams are checked by OMR machines, which scan the filled bubbles.
    
- **OCR (Optical Character Recognition):** Reads printed text and converts it into editable text (e.g., converting a scan of a book page into a Word document).
    
- **MICR (Magnetic Ink Character Recognition):** Used exclusively in **Banking**. It reads the special magnetic numbers at the bottom of cheques (Cheque number, routing number) to prevent fraud.
    

**4. Audio/Visual Input**

- **Microphone:** Converts sound waves into electrical audio signals (Digital Audio).
    
- **Webcam:** Captures video input.
    

---

#### B. Output Devices (Result Presentation)

An Output Device receives processed data from the computer and converts it into a human-perceptible form (text, audio, video).

1. Monitors (Visual Display Unit - VDU)

Produces a "Soft Copy" (temporary, intangible output).

- **LCD (Liquid Crystal Display):** Uses liquid crystals to block or pass light.
    
- **LED (Light Emitting Diode):** An advanced version of LCD that uses LEDs for backlighting. It is thinner, brighter, and more energy-efficient.
    
- **User Context:** Your laptop screen is likely a high-quality **IPS (In-Plane Switching) Panel**. It refreshes dozens of times per second (Hertz) to create smooth motion, essential for the high-performance tasks your i7 processor handles.
    

2. Printers

Produces a "Hard Copy" (permanent, physical output on paper).

- **Impact Printers:** They strike a ribbon against the paper (physically touching it).
    
    - _Example:_ **Dot Matrix Printer**. Noisy and low quality, but cheap. Still used in Bangladesh for printing invoices in wholesale markets or bank statements (carbon copies).
        
- **Non-Impact Printers:** They do _not_ strike the paper.
    
    - _Inkjet:_ Sprays microscopic drops of ink. Good for color photos, expensive ink.
        
    - _Laser:_ Uses a laser beam and static electricity to fuse toner (powder) onto paper. Fast, high quality, and economical for bulk text printing (standard in university labs).
        

**3. Audio/Video Output**

- **Speakers/Headphones:** Converts digital audio data back into sound waves.
    
- **Projector:** Projects the computer image onto a large wall or screen. Commonly used in your lecture halls at Khulna University.

### _Additional Necessary Information:_ Dual-Purpose Devices (I/O)

_This category is often missed but is technically critical. Some devices perform BOTH input and output functions._

1. **Touchscreen:** It displays information (Output) and registers your finger's touch as a command (Input). (e.g., Smartphones, Tablet Kiosks).
    
2. **Modem / Network Card:** Receives data from the internet (Input) and sends your data out (Output).
    
3. **Headsets:** Combine a speaker (Output) and a microphone (Input).

### Important Notes & Exam Tips

- **Resolution Metrics:**
    
    - **Monitors** are measured in **Pixels** (e.g., 1920x1080).
        
    - **Printers** are measured in **DPI** (Dots Per Inch). Higher DPI = sharper print.
        
- **Soft Copy vs. Hard Copy:**
    
    - _Soft Copy:_ Electronic, modifiable, displayed on a screen (e.g., a PDF file).
        
    - _Hard Copy:_ Physical, permanent, printed on paper (e.g., a printed report).
        
- **The "MICR" Tip:** If you see a question about "Banking automation" or "Cheque processing," the answer is almost always **MICR**.
    
- **Bangladeshi Application:** In your "Cyber Ethics" or general knowledge section, remember that **Biometric Scanners** (fingerprint scanners) are now standard Input devices in Bangladesh for SIM card registration and NID verification.
