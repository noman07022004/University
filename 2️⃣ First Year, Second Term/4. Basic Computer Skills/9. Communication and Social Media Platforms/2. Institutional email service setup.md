## 9.2 Institutional Email Service Setup ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (4/5):** Important practical knowledge. Without this, you miss out on student discounts (GitHub Pack, Office 365) and cannot communicate officially.
    
- **Exam (2/5):** Low Frequency. You might be asked "Why is institutional email important?" or "Steps to configure an email client," but rarely the specific administrative setup process of the university.
    

---

### Getting Your Digital ID

Setting up your **Khulna University Institutional Email** (ending in `@ku.ac.bd` or `@discipline.ku.ac.bd`) is an administrative process rather than a purely technical one. Unlike creating a Gmail account which takes 2 minutes online, this requires verification of your student status by the university's **ICT Cell**.

This email address is your "Digital Identity Card." It proves to the world (and software companies) that you are a genuine student of Khulna University.

#### Step-by-Step Setup Process (Khulna University Context)

Based on the official procedure managed by the **ICT Cell** of Khulna University, here is how you obtain your credentials:

**1. Obtain the Application Form**

- You need the **"Web Mail Requisition Form"** (Application for University Email ID).
    
- **Source:** You can download this PDF from the Khulna University website (under your Discipline's "Downloadable Forms" section, e.g., CSE or English Discipline pages). Alternatively, the **ICT Cell** (Administrative Building) or your Department Office often keeps hard copies.
    

**2. Fill Out the Form**

- The form has two parts: **Personal Copy** and **ICT Cell Copy**. You must fill both.
    
- **Required Data:**
    
    - **Full Name:** As per your registration card.
        
    - **Student ID:** Your specific KU ID (e.g., 190201...).
        
    - **Discipline:** Computer Science and Engineering.
        
    - **Desired Mail Address:** Usually follows the format `firstname.lastname@ku.ac.bd` or `id@ku.ac.bd`.
        
    - **Phone Number:** For password recovery contact.
        

**3. Verification & Submission**

- **Signature:** You must sign the form.
    
- **Recommendation:** Usually, you need the signature/seal of your **Head of Discipline** (Head of CSE) to verify you are a current student.
    
- **Submit:** Take the completed form to the **ICT Cell** located in the **Administrative Building** (usually Room 337 or 302).
    

**4. Activation & Login**

- **Processing Time:** The ICT Cell engineers will manually create your ID. This may take 1-3 working days.
    
- **Credentials:** You will receive a default password (often sent to your secondary email or phone).
    
- **First Login:**
    
    - Go to the **Khulna University Website** (ku.ac.bd) or the direct Webmail link provided (often a Gmail-based interface or dedicated portal).
        
    - Enter your new `@ku.ac.bd` address and the temporary password.
        
    - **Change Password:** You _must_ change the password immediately upon first login for security.
        

#### Troubleshooting Common Issues

- **"User Not Found":** If you cannot log in, your profile might not be updated in the central database. Contact your Discipline office to ensure your data was sent to the ICT Cell.
    
- **Password Reset:** If you forget your password, you cannot just click "Forgot Password" like Facebook. You often need to fill out the same form again, checking the **"Password Reset"** box, and submit it to the ICT Cell.

### _Additional Necessary Information:_ Configuration on Devices

_Once you have the ID, you shouldn't just check it on the web. You should add it to your apps._

**Configuring on Your Laptop (Windows 11 Mail / Outlook):**

1. Open **Mail** or **Outlook**.
    
2. Click **Add Account** > **Google** (Since KU mail is often hosted via Google Workspace for Education).
    
3. Enter your full `studentID@ku.ac.bd` address.
    
4. It will redirect you to the university login page (if applicable) or ask for your password.
    
5. Now, your official emails will pop up in your Windows notifications, ensuring you never miss a class notice.

### Important Notes & Exam Tips

- **Official Use Only:** This email is monitored. Do not use it for signing up on questionable websites, gaming, or piracy sites. It is for academic and professional use only.
    
- **Data Backup:** When you graduate, this email might be deactivated. Always back up important Drive files to a personal Gmail account before leaving the university.
    
- **Exam Q:** "What are the advantages of an institutional email?"
    
    - **Answer:** Authenticity (proves identity), Professionalism (looks better on CVs), Access to Academic Resources (journals/software), and Security (managed by IT professionals).
