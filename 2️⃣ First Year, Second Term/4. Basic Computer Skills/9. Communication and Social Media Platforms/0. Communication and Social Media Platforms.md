## 9. Communication and Social Media Platforms ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (5/5):** In the modern university ecosystem, these are your primary tools for survival. You receive official notices via Email, attend classes via Zoom/Meet, and collaborate with peers via Social Media.
    
- **Exam (4/5):** Frequent. Questions often cover "Difference between CC and BCC," "Benefits of Cloud Computing," or "Impact of Social Media on Education."
    

---

### The Digital Agora

**Communication Platforms** are software tools that facilitate the exchange of information between two or more parties. **Social Media**, a subset of this, refers to interactive technologies that allow the creation and sharing of information, ideas, interests, and other forms of expression via virtual communities and networks.

The shift from physical notice boards to digital platforms has revolutionized academia. As a student at Khulna University, your "attendance" is often digital (Zoom), your "submission" is electronic (Email/Classroom), and your "group study" is virtual (Messenger/WhatsApp).

#### 1. Email Services (The Official Channel)

**Electronic Mail (Email)** is the method of exchanging messages between people using electronic devices. It is the gold standard for **formal** communication. While you might chat with friends on Facebook, you _must_ email your professors.

- **Structure:** An email consists of a **Header** (To, Subject, Date) and a **Body** (Message).
    
- **Institutional Email Setup:**
    
    - Most universities, including Khulna University, provide students with an official email address ending in `.ac.bd` or `.edu`.
        
    - **Why it matters:** This isn't just for prestige. An institutional email acts as a digital ID card. It grants you free access to paid software like **Microsoft Office 365**, **Canva Pro**, **GitHub Student Developer Pack**, and research journals that usually cost money.
        
    - _Setup Tip:_ You typically need to apply through the university's ICT cell. Once active, link this email to your Google Scholar and ResearchGate profiles to prove your affiliation.
        

#### 2. Video Conferencing (The Virtual Classroom)

This technology allows users in different locations to hold face-to-face meetings. Since the pandemic, these have become synonymous with "Classrooms."

- **Zoom:**
    
    - _Features:_ Famous for "Breakout Rooms" (splitting a large class into small study groups) and "Virtual Backgrounds."
        
    - _Limitation:_ The free version has a 40-minute limit for group meetings.
        
- **Google Meet:**
    
    - _Features:_ Deeply integrated with Google Classroom and Calendar. Live captioning is excellent.
        
    - _Benefit:_ It is generally lighter on resources (RAM) than Zoom, which is good if your internet is unstable.
        

#### 3. Social Media Platforms (The Informal Network)

- **Facebook:**
    
    - In the **Bangladeshi Context**, Facebook is not just for fun; it is an essential utility.
        
    - **Uses:** University "Confessions" pages, Departmental Notice groups, and "F-Commerce" (buying/selling via Facebook).
        
    - **Academic Warning:** While useful for peer support, information found in student Facebook groups is _unofficial_. Always verify dates and notices with the official website or email.
        

#### 4. Cloud Computing (The Storage & Power)

**Cloud Computing** is the delivery of computing services—including servers, storage, databases, networking, software, and intelligence—over the Internet ("the cloud").

- **Concept:** Instead of saving a file on your laptop's hard drive (Local Storage), you save it on Google's massive servers (Cloud Storage).
    
- **Benefits:**
    
    - **Accessibility:** You can open your assignment on your phone, library PC, or laptop.
        
    - **Backup:** If your laptop is stolen or crashes, your data is safe.
        
    - **Collaboration:** Multiple students can type in the same Google Doc simultaneously.
        
- **Major Providers:** **Google Drive** (15GB Free), **Microsoft OneDrive** (Integrated into your Windows 11), **Dropbox**.
    

#### 5. Scanning Applications (Digitizing the Physical)

Since you cannot shove a physical paper into a laptop screen, you need **Scanning Apps**. These use your phone's camera and AI to:

1. Detect the edges of the paper.
    
2. Correct the perspective (flatten the image).
    
3. Enhance the text contrast (whitening the background).
    
4. Convert it into a **PDF**.
    

- **Common Tools:** **CamScanner** (Popular but has watermarks), **Adobe Scan** (Professional, OCR capable), **Google Drive App** (Has a hidden built-in scanner).

### _Additional Necessary Information:_ Email Protocols (CC vs. BCC)

_This is the single most frequent technical question from this topic._

When sending an email, you have three address fields:

1. **To:** The main recipient (who is expected to reply/act).
    
2. **CC (Carbon Copy):** Secondary recipients. They receive a copy for information, but are not expected to act. _Everyone can see who is in the CC list._
    
    - _Usage:_ Emailing your Professor (`To`) and copying your Class Representative (`CC`) so they know you sent it.
        
3. **BCC (Blind Carbon Copy):** Hidden recipients. They receive a copy, but _no one else knows_ they received it.
    
    - _Usage:_ A teacher emailing exam grades to 50 students. They put the students in `BCC` so students cannot see each other's email addresses (Privacy).

### Important Notes & Exam Tips

- **Netiquette:** In exams, if asked about "Email Etiquette," mention:
    
    - Always include a clear **Subject Line**.
        
    - Use a formal salutation (e.g., "Dear Sir," not "Hey").
        
    - Don't write in ALL CAPS (it looks like shouting).
        
- **SaaS (Software as a Service):** If asked for an example of Cloud Computing, **Google Docs** or **Gmail** are examples of SaaS. You use the software via the internet without installing it.
    
- **Security Tip:** Never share your Zoom/Meet link publicly on Facebook. This leads to "Zoombombing," where strangers join and disrupt the class.
