
## Association ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (⭐ 5/5):** Essential for understanding modern society. As society moves from _Gemeinschaft_ (Community) to _Gesellschaft_ (Association), understanding this concept explains how modern legal and economic systems function.
    
- **Exam (⭐ 4/5):** A frequent topic for "Distinguish between" questions. The difference between "Association" and "Institution" is a classic exam trap.
    

---

### 1. Definition and Meaning

In modern society, we cannot achieve everything alone, nor does the "Community" (which covers _all_ of life) solve every specific problem. Therefore, we form specific groups to achieve specific goals. These are **Associations**.

Definition:

An association is a group of people organized for the pursuit of a specific purpose or a limited number of purposes.

**Scholarly Definitions:**

- **MacIver and Page:** _"An association is a group organized for the pursuit of an interest or a group of interests in common."_
    
- **G.D.H. Cole:** _"Association is any group of persons pursuing a common purpose or a system or aggregation of purposes by a course of cooperative action."_
    

Key Concept:

While a Community is natural and covers your whole life, an Association is artificial and covers only a part of your life (e.g., your job, your sport, your political view).

### 2. Essential Characteristics

1. **Group of People:** It is a concrete group you can see and count.
    
2. **Common Interest:** People join because they want the same thing (e.g., A Trade Union is formed to get higher wages).
    
3. **Cooperative Spirit:** Members must work together. If they fight, the association dissolves.
    
4. **Organization:** It has a structure—President, Secretary, Treasurer, etc.
    
5. **Regulation (Rules):** It operates based on written or unwritten laws (By-laws, Constitution).
    
6. **Voluntary Membership:** Unlike a caste or family (where you are born into it), you generally _choose_ to join an association.
    

### 3. Association vs. Community (The Exam Distinction)

This is a standard exam question.

|**Feature**|**Association**|**Community**|
|---|---|---|
|**Origin**|Created deliberately/artificially.|Grows spontaneously/naturally.|
|**Goal**|Specific/Partial interest (e.g., Playing Cricket).|General/Total interest (Living life).|
|**Membership**|Voluntary (You join it).|Compulsory/Involuntary (You are born in it).|
|**Sentiment**|Based on rules and cooperation.|Based on "We-feeling" and emotion.|
|**Stability**|Can be temporary (dissolves after goal is met).|Relatively permanent.|

### 4. Association vs. Institution (The Conceptual Trap)

This is where students often lose marks. In English, we call a university an "institution." In Sociology, we must be precise.

- **Association** = The **People** (The organized group).
    
- **Institution** = The **Rules** (The mode of procedure).
    

**MacIver’s Golden Rule:**

> _"We belong to associations, but we do not belong to institutions."_

**Examples:**

- **The Family:**
    
    - As a group of parents/kids = **Association**.
        
    - As the system of marriage/inheritance rules = **Institution**.
        
- **The College:**
    
    - The teachers and students = **Association**.
        
    - The system of lectures, exams, and degrees = **Institution**.
        
- **The State:**
    
    - The Government/Citizens = **Association**.
        
    - The Constitution/Law = **Institution**.
        

### 5. Bangladeshi Context: Examples

To show "Application" in your exam, use these examples:

1. **Professional Associations:** _Bangladesh Bar Council_ (for lawyers), _Bangladesh Medical Association (BMA)_.
    
    - _Purpose:_ Protecting the professional interests of members.
        
2. **Economic Associations:** _BGMEA_ (Garment Manufacturers).
    
    - _Purpose:_ Lobbying the government for trade benefits.
        
3. **Student Associations:** _Dhaka University Central Students' Union (DUCSU)_ or Khulna University equivalent.
    
    - _Purpose:_ Representing student rights.
        

---

### 📝 Important Notes & Exam Tips

- **MacIver's Quote:** I cannot stress this enough—memorize _"We belong to associations, but we do not belong to institutions."_ If you write this in an exam, the examiner immediately knows you understand the core concept.
    
- **Legal Relevance:** As a law student, note that **Associations** are "Legal Persons" (Corporate Personality). They can sue and be sued. A "Community" generally cannot be sued because it is not a legal entity.
    
- **Evolution:** You can mention that as Bangladesh modernizes (Urbanization), the role of _Community_ (Samaj) is decreasing, and the role of _Associations_ (NGOs, Clubs) is increasing. This is a great point for "Social Change."
