## 4.10 Legal Effects of a Valid Marriage ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Rating Justification:**

- **Understanding (⭐ 4/5):** A valid marriage is not just a ceremony; it is a legal trigger that instantly alters the status, rights, and obligations of both parties. Understanding these effects is essential to grasp the concept of "marital status" in law.
    
- **Exam (⭐ 4/5):** While less common as a standalone essay, these points are the _ingredients_ for answering almost any problem question involving maintenance, inheritance, or guardianship.
    

---

### Introduction

Once the sacred rites of marriage (specifically the _Saptapadi_) are completed, the couple undergoes a transformation in legal status. They cease to be two separate individuals and become husband and wife. In the eyes of Hindu Law, the wife is considered the _Ardhangini_ (half of the husband's body). This spiritual union creates a bundle of legal rights and duties that are enforceable in a court of law.

### 1. Conjugal Rights (Right to Society)

The most immediate effect of marriage is the right to **Cohabitation**.

- **The Right:** Each spouse is entitled to the society, comfort, and companionship of the other. The wife has a duty to live with her husband in his home, and the husband has a duty to live with his wife.
    
- **The Remedy:** If one spouse abandons the other without "reasonable excuse" (e.g., cruelty, disease), the aggrieved party can file a suit for **Restitution of Conjugal Rights** in the civil court.
    
    - _Note:_ The court can order the spouse to return, but it cannot physically force them to live together. Disobedience of the decree may lead to property attachment, but not arrest.
        

### 2. Right to Maintenance

- **Husband's Duty:** The husband is under a personal, absolute legal obligation to maintain his wife. This includes providing food, clothing, shelter, medical care, and other necessities consistent with his social status.
    
- **Condition of Chastity:** The wife’s right to maintenance is conditional upon her remaining **chaste** (faithful). An unchaste wife loses her right to be maintained.
    
- **Separate Residence:** Generally, she must live with him to claim maintenance. However, she can claim "Separate Maintenance" if he is guilty of cruelty, has another wife living, or suffers from a virulent disease.
    

### 3. Legitimacy of Children

- **Presumption:** Marriage confers the status of **Legitimacy** on children born during the wedlock.
    
- **Legal Consequence:** Only legitimate children have the right to inherit the father's ancestral property and offer _Pinda_ (funeral cakes). Illegitimate children (in the higher three castes) are generally entitled only to maintenance, not inheritance.
    

### 4. Change of Gotra and Domicile

- **Gotra (Lineage):** Upon the completion of the 7th step, the wife legally leaves her father’s _Gotra_ and enters her husband’s _Gotra_. She becomes part of his family lineage.
    
- **Domicile:** The wife legally acquires the domicile of her husband. Even if she is a minor living with her parents, her legal home is presumed to be where her husband lives.
    

### 5. Rights of Inheritance

- **Dayabhaga Rule:** In Bangladesh, a lawfully wedded wife is a potential heir. If the husband dies without leaving any male issue (son, grandson, great-grandson), the widow succeeds to his property.
    
- **Nature of Estate:** She inherits a "Widow's Estate" (Limited Estate)—she can enjoy the income for life but cannot sell the property (corpus) except for strict legal necessity.
    

### 6. Guardianship

- **Minor Wife:** If the bride is a minor (which, as discussed, is valid though punishable), the husband becomes her natural legal guardian, superseding her father.
    
- **Offspring:** The husband is the natural guardian of the minor children born of the marriage.

### Important Notes & Exam Tips

> **The "Restitution" Concept:** In exams, if a problem says _"The wife left the husband because he beat her. Can he force her to return?"_
> 
> - **Answer:** No. While he has a right to conjugal society, "Cruelty" is a valid defense against a suit for Restitution of Conjugal Rights.
>     

> **Status of Wife:** Use the phrase **"Fusion of Personalities"** to describe the legal effect. In ancient law, the husband and wife are one person, and strictly speaking, that person is the husband.

> **Maintenance vs. Inheritance:**
> 
> - **Maintenance:** Available while the husband is alive (and from his estate after death).
>     
> - **Inheritance:** Arises only _after_ the husband's death.
