Course title: Equity, Trust and Specific Relief
Course Status: Core
Credit: 3.0
Prerequisite(s): None
Rationale: This course is designed to introduce students with the principles of equity and equitable rights and legal provisions regarding trust and specific relief and their application in Bangladesh.
### Course Contents
#### Section A
1. Concept of Equity: Common law, Origin and growth of equity, Nature and definition, Courts of Equity: Composition, powers and functions, Application of equity, Jurisdiction in Bangladesh;
2. Law and Equity: Relationship between law and equity, Equitable estate and legal estate, Maxims of equity;
3. Equitable Remedies: Nature and scope of equitable remedies, Specific performance, Injunction, Receiver;
#### Section B
4. Trust: History and growth of trust, Nature and definition, Classification, Creation and extinction of trust, Remedies for breach of trust, Doctrine of cypress;
5. Trustee and Beneficiary: Appointment and removal, Rights and duties, Powers and functions, Liabilities and disabilities, Limitation, Rights and liabilities of the beneficiaries;
6. Laws Relating to Specific Relief: Specific relief, Specific performance of contract, Rectification and cancellation of instruments, Rescission of contracts, Declaratory decrees, Appointment of receivers, Injunction.

### Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
Upon successful completion of the course, the students will be able to:
1. understand the concepts, principles and rules relating to equity, trust and Specific Relief;
2. apply and evaluate the principles of equity, and the interaction between common law and equity;
3. demonstrate an understanding of the development of the existing law of equity and trust;
4. engage in critical discussion on equity and trust as legal devices focusing on real life situation.
