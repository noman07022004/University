Course Code: 0421 28 Law 2103
Year: Second
Term: First
Course title: Equity, Trust and Specific Relief
Course Status: Core
Credit: 3.0
Prerequisite(s): None
Rationale: This course is designed to introduce students with the principles of equity and equitable rights and legal provisions regarding trust and specific relief and their application in Bangladesh.
## Course Contents

### Section A
1. Concept of Equity:
    1. Common law,
    2. Origin and growth of equity,
    3. Nature and definition,
    4. Courts of Equity: Composition, powers and functions,
    5. Application of equity,
    6. Jurisdiction in Bangladesh;

2. Law and Equity:
    1. Relationship between law and equity,
    2. Equitable estate and legal estate,
    3. Maxims of equity;

3. Equitable Remedies:
    1. Nature and scope of equitable remedies,
    2. Specific performance,
    3. Injunction,
    4. Receiver;


### Section B
4. Trust:
    1. History and growth of trust,
    2. Nature and definition,
    3. Classification,
    4. Creation and extinction of trust,
    5. Remedies for breach of trust,
    6. Doctrine of cypress;

5. Trustee and Beneficiary:
    1. Appointment and removal,
    2. Rights and duties,
    3. Powers and functions,
    4. Liabilities and disabilities,
    5. Limitation,
    6. Rights and liabilities of the beneficiaries;

6. Laws Relating to Specific Relief:
    1. Specific relief,
    2. Specific performance of contract,
    3. Rectification and cancellation of instruments,
    4. Rescission of contracts,
    5. Declaratory decrees,
    6. Appointment of receivers,
    7. Injunction.
